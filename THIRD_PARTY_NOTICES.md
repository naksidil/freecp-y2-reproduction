# Attribution and redistribution scope

This repository includes attributed adaptations and source snapshots used for an individual YZM5110 research project.

- **FreeCP**: Q. Chen et al., ICCV 2025, https://github.com/chenqi1126/FreeCP . Preserve the upstream MIT text in `freecp_stage*/FreeCP_LICENSE.txt` / `UPSTREAM_LICENSE.txt` and the source attribution headers. The reproduced revision is recorded in README and the protocol files.
- **PAMR**: N. Araslanov and S. Roth, CVPR 2020, https://github.com/visinf/1-stage-wseg . The imported distribution and its source hash are documented in the stage06 provenance; the original and distribution license files are preserved there.
- CLIP, SCLIP, MMSegmentation, MMCV and other dependencies retain their own licenses. They are installed or obtained from the attributed sources by the setup workflow.
- PASCAL VOC images/labels and CLIP model weights are not included. Obtain them from the documented providers and comply with their terms. Selected saved prediction maps are model outputs, not redistributed VOC photographs or ground-truth files.

There is no blanket relicensing of third-party code. Original project additions are provided for inspection and reproduction of this course submission; no separate broad license is asserted here.
